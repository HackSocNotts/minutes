\section{Reports}

\subsection{HackSoc Year in Review - Aaron Osher}
\begin{hiddensubitems}
	\item
	Year-on-year Revenue is \pounds25,000 with the society spending \pounds18,000
		\subitem
		margins accounted for \pounds8,500 in revenue, and spent \pounds4,000
		\subitem
		HackNotts 2019 accounted or \pounds12,500 in revenue, and spent \pounds11,000
		\subitem
		HHEU Unconference accounted for \pounds2,500 in revenue and expenditure
	\item
	HackNotts 2019 had over 150 hackers, 25 staff, and 20 sponsor reps, and judges
		\subitem
		The even has continued the trend of exceeding past years, and the revamp to the organisational structure for 2019, has put the event on target to do the same
	\item
	Bruce Bernard II, the giant teddy-bear, has greatly improved our social media presence in the UK hacker scene
		\subitem The bear has also attended four hackathons on two continents
	\item
	We met our goal of running at least 2 events per week throughout the year
		\subitem
		We initially found ourselves battling some welfare related issues, and streamlined our events process in December to resolve this
	\item
	Member engagement has generally been up over the last year
		\subitem
		We don't keep records, but we've also noticed generally a more diverse range of attendees at events over the last year
	\item
	Weekend workshops replacing series has worked incredibly well
		\subitem
		Moving our multi-week series to weekend events has resulted in better attendance numbers, and more engaged attendees
	\item
	We've continued to maintain our relationship with Tech Nottingham
	\item
	Speaker events
		\subitem
		We brought in 20 external speakers this year, a substantial increase over the 12 we had in 2018/19
		\subitem
		Our multi-speaker events were huge successes
	\item
	External Hackathons
		\subitem
		In January we started going to Hackathons as a society for the first time in years
		\subitem
		We've smashed attendee numbers at events like CovHack, making up 20\% of the event's attendees
		\subitem
		We've also subsidised hired transport for the first time to support the significant engagement
\end{hiddensubitems}
