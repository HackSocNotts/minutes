\section{margins 2019 retrospective}
\begin{items}
	\item Deliver After Action Review - AD
		\subitem Link to AAR - https://hacksoc.net/margins-aar
	\item SWOT Analysis - AD
		\subitem Strengths
			\subsubitem Venue was really good - Gender Neutral Toilets, Lots of space
			\subsubitem Atmosphere of the event was very positive - cosy
			\subsubitem Speakers were all very good. Engaging talks, relevant topics.
		\subitem Weakness
			\subsubitem We had 50\% attrition
			\subsubitem Running event out of term may have affected turnout
			\subsubitem Over provisioning of food and swag
			\subsubitem Self-care workshop (afternoon) had a lot of drop outs - only a few attendees
		\subitem Opportunities
			\subsubitem Lots of space to grow in the venue
			\subsubitem This year was successful so we can build on top of it
			\subsubitem Amy's talk saved us from a dropped-out speaker
		\subitem Threats
			\subsubitem Due Diligence with Scholarships - \pounds112 of scholarship issued to a potentially fraudulent application
			\subsubitem Due Diligence with suppliers (Caterer fell through, two swag suppliers were defunct) \textit{Aim for convenience not localness with swag suppliers, they all outsource anyways}
			\subsubitem Speaker drop out - Contingencies were already there to an extent
			\subsubitem Organising practices for the event should be formalised and document to avoid bus factor in the future - perhaps in the HackSoc internal Wiki
	\item Preparation
		\subitem Issues with suppliers were handled well, replaced promptly
			\subsubitem Getting PLI, Food Safety Certificate should be one of the first things to get from caters
			\subsubitem HackSoc's policy for caterers is nothing more than a quote negotiated prior to receipt of all required documentation - If caterer wants your business or is worth your time they'll be prompt
		\subitem Volunteers used effectively outside of the event - Preparing swag, food etc.
		\subitem Speakers/Workshop Leaders Getting designated team member was positive feedback
		\subitem Risk assessment/Health and Safety Concerns - AO
			\subsubitem [Hot Water Urn, Non-individual coffee, fresh produce] these are all high-risk activities that weren't in the risk assessment
			\subsubitem SU is turning Health and Safety training into an online Moodle course for 2019/20, this should be reviewed by organisers for next year
		\subitem Some incidents where lead-times were too tight, e.g. roller banners, caterer, hats
	\item During the Event
		\subitem Issues with Estates/Security was handled well and promptly
			\subsubitem Catering location changed for the better
		\subitem Over-loaded with volunteers
			\subsubitem Lots of idle time
			\subsubitem Issues with volunteers cliquing (specifically HackSoc volunteers)
		\subitem Catering went well
			\subsubitem Extra portions at lunch are an unknown contributor to positive feedback
			\subsubitem Final position of food was more optimal than originally planned
		\subitem Terrance access was popular
		\subitem Schedule Changes - VS
		\subitem People didn't necessarily know who the sponsors were, how to interact with them
			\subsubitem Sponsors were put in corners - Refinitiv away from the rest of the venue
		\subitem Heavy attrition after lunch
			\subsubitem Lunch was too long
			\subsubitem In future it may make sense to make lunch 30 minutes (split in two slots?)
			\subsubitem Weren't enough activities for people to do/mingle around during lunch
	\item Recommendations
		\subitem Marketing Language - VS
			\subsubitem Some people didn't comfortable identifying themselves as marginalised, changing the language may be worthwhile.
		\subitem Pre-event communication
			\subsubitem Better communicate 
		\subitem Finances/Surplus Funds
			\subsubitem Would have been good to inform applicable individuals about opportunities to expense local travel after the event
			\subsubitem Delay paying speakers until after Refinitiv money has cleared
			\subsubitem Look into what is owed to Amy from WiT17 and reimburse
			\subsubitem Invest most excess funds into future margins/inspireWiT
		\subitem Investigation into errant travel scholarship
			\subsubitem Post mortem will be distributed and added to the HackSoc Internal Wiki
	\item Next Steps - Future margins
		\subitem Re-use of existing document and forms - VS
			\subsubitem Code of Conduct can be re-used for next year
		\subitem Catering next year - Perhaps use one of the local caters already involved in BME
		\subitem Refinitiv has expressed wanting to work together on further workshop and activities in this space
		\subitem Clarify zero-tolerance policy on intolerance
		\subitem Should 2020 be bigger?
			\subsubitem Should continue to aim for 100 attendees
			\subsubitem We should keep the cosy feel of the event
		\subitem April 2020? That way it is still nice out but it's also during term
		\subitem Make some sort of crafting thing to do that get's made as a group in the space
		\subitem Catering
			\subsubitem Juicy Jackets was not first choice, presumably this will hold true in the future
			\subsubitem Breakfast - Lots of people went after the bars and not fruit (perhaps because we're approaching an age where people don't like eating an orange in public)
		\subitem Swag
			\subsubitem We had a lot more swag than needed, most can be reused to an extent
			\subsubitem Bags - felt a bit wasteful when putting stuff away at the end - perhaps a stack of swag as a solution in future
			\subsubitem Some better communication about "taking a bag" would help resolve the large amount of them left over
		\subitem Volunteer Hats
			\subsubitem They didn't fit particularly 
			\subsubitem Should keep the head idea (Bandanna, Visor, Beanie?)
			\subsubitem People were too comfortable to take them off indoors
		\subitem Speakers
			\subsubitem Have a better way to notify speakers when they have five minutes remaining etc.
		\subitem Target Audiences in future
			\subsubitem Women in Tech Community
			\subsubitem BME Communities
			\subsubitem Latinx?
			\subsubitem Not all groups like using negative (marginalised/discriminated against) language, because they can be more about empowerment
			\subsubitem Expanding the "work with concept" to avoid exclusivity of those that aren't in designated "tech jobs"
		\subitem Marketting
			\subsubitem Make it clearer that it's not just for people who work in Tech
			\subsubitem Frame vegan free food as just free food
			\subsubitem Using photos on marketing materials
			\subsubitem Promote free food more in general
\end{items}
